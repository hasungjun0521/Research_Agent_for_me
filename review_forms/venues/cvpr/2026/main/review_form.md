# CVPR 2026 Main Review Form

## Metadata

- Venue: CVPR
- Year: 2026
- Track: main
- Stage: review
- Reviewer name:

## Summary

Briefly summarize the paper and its contributions. This is not the place to critique the paper; authors should generally agree with a well-written summary.

Required. Write one paragraph with at least 5 to 7 sentences. Do not use bullet points.

## Soundness

Assign a numerical rating for the soundness of the technical claims, experimental and research methodology, and whether the central claims are adequately supported with evidence.

Choose one:

- 4: excellent
- 3: good
- 2: fair
- 1: poor

## Presentation

Assign a numerical rating for presentation quality, including writing style, clarity, and contextualization relative to prior work.

Choose one:

- 4: excellent
- 3: good
- 2: fair
- 1: poor

## Contribution

Assign a numerical rating for the quality of the overall contribution this paper makes to the research area. Consider whether the questions are important, whether the paper brings significant originality of ideas and/or execution, and whether the results are valuable to share with the broader community.

Choose one:

- 4: excellent
- 3: good
- 2: fair
- 1: poor

## Strengths

Provide a substantive assessment of the paper's strengths, touching on originality, quality, clarity, and significance. Originality may come from a new definition or problem formulation, creative combinations of existing ideas, application to a new domain, or removing limitations from prior results.

## Weaknesses

Provide a substantive assessment of the paper's weaknesses. Focus on constructive and actionable insights for how the work could improve toward its stated goals. Be specific and avoid generic remarks. If the contribution lacks novelty, provide references and explanation as evidence; if experiments are insufficient, explain why and exactly what is missing.

## Questions

List and carefully describe questions and suggestions for the authors. Focus on issues where a response from the authors can change your opinion, clarify confusion, or address a limitation.

## Rating

Provide an overall score for this submission. The expected acceptance rate is around 20 to 25 percent; when reviewing 4 to 5 papers, an accept should correspond to one of the strongest papers in that set.

Choose one:

- 10: strong accept, should be highlighted at the conference
- 8: accept, good paper
- 6: marginally above the acceptance threshold
- 5: marginally below the acceptance threshold
- 3: reject, not good enough
- 1: strong reject

## Confidence

Choose one:

- 5: Absolutely certain
- 4: Confident but not absolutely certain
- 3: Fairly confident
- 2: Willing to defend assessment, but likely uncertainty
- 1: Unable to assess and AC should seek another opinion

# ECCV 2026 Main Review Form

## Paper Summary

Summarize in your own words the paper, its key ideas, its contributions, and their significance. This summary helps the AC and authors understand the rest of your review and be confident that you understand the paper.

Required. Write 5 to 7 sentences in paragraph form. Do not use bullet points.

## Paper Strengths

List anything that could be of interest to readers and explain why. Consider what you learned from the paper, whether there is a key idea that could inspire others, and whether the paper presents significant results likely to interest the community.

Required. If using bullet points, use first-level bullets only and write 2 to 3 items.

## Major Weaknesses

Describe as carefully as possible the weaknesses that could justify rejection. Major weaknesses include insufficient evidence for claims, lack of novelty with citations, or claims that are not meaningful or of possible interest to the community.

Remember that a submission is not required to compare to closed-source papers or papers only on arXiv under the stated PAMI-TC motions.

Required. If using bullet points, use first-level bullets only and write at least 3 items.

## Minor Weaknesses

Mention weaknesses that are easy to fix in a revision, such as occasional typographic errors or minor unclear points.

Required. If using bullet points, use first-level bullets only.

## Preliminary Recommendation

Choose one:

- 6: Accept
- 5: Weak Accept
- 4: Borderline Accept
- 3: Borderline Reject
- 2: Weak Reject
- 1: Reject

Required.

## Justification For Recommendation And Suggestions For Rebuttal

Carefully balance strengths and weaknesses to justify your recommendation. Explain what should be clarified to change your opinion. You may request clarifications, additional illustrations, or small experiments that could be reasonably run during rebuttal with academic resources. Do not request substantial additional experiments for rebuttal or penalize for lack of additional experiments.

Required. If using bullet points, use first-level bullets only.

## Confidence Level

Choose one:

- 5: Expert
- 4: High Confidence
- 3: Moderate Confidence
- 2: Low Confidence
- 1: Not Confident

Required.
